# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be decent to each other. That's really it.

---

## Why This Matters

WattWise is a small project, and I want it to be a space where people feel comfortable contributing, asking questions, or just hanging out. This code of conduct isn't about rules and regulations—it's about establishing some ground rules so everyone can get along.

---

## What We Expect

- **Treat people like humans** — We're all here because we care about energy tracking (yes, that's a thing). That shared interest should be enough to build on.
- **Be respectful** — You won't agree with everyone, and that's fine. Disagreement is healthy. Disrespect isn't.
- **Give constructive feedback** — "This is wrong" isn't helpful. "Here's what I'd change and why" gets results.
- **Own your mistakes** — We all mess up. Own it, learn from it, move on.

---

## What We Don't Tolerate

- Harassment of any kind
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Sharing private information without permission
- Anything that would make someone feel unsafe

If you see something, say something. Better to address an issue early than let it fester.

---

## How We Handle Problems

If someone violates this code of conduct:

1. A conversation will happen. Usually that's enough.
2. If it continues, there may be a formal warning.
3. Repeat offenders might get banned from contributing.

We're not looking to kick people out. But we will if we have to.

---
